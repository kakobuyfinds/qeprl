03 / Kakobuy shopping guide

Turn a product link into a useful decision.

A repeatable research process for sneakers, clothing, and accessories.

01

Build a focused shortlist

Choose one category and record the exact listing and selected variant. Keep color, size, and seller notes together so similar products do not get mixed up.

02

Compare fit, not labels

For Kakobuy clothing finds, compare garment measurements with a piece you already own. For shoes, use the listing’s size chart and your foot measurements.

03

Read photos critically

Use available Kakobuy QC photos to inspect visible details, selected colors, and apparent condition. Images cannot prove authenticity or reveal every material issue.

04

Check the total before buying

Confirm current ordering support, delivery options, fees, and return terms with the relevant service. Treat a saved product price as a reference until rechecked.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product-research list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Useful fields include the product URL, category, listed price, measurements, and QC observations. A list does not guarantee availability, quality, authenticity, or agent support.

Is this the official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide that links to CAFANS. It is not operated by or endorsed by Kakobuy or the brands named on this page.

How do I use Kakobuy spreadsheet finds?

Choose a category, open a relevant listing, and record the product details in your own shortlist. Check the selected variant, measurements, current price, seller information, and any available QC photos before deciding whether to order.

Can QC photos prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help you inspect visible details and apparent condition, but they do not establish authenticity. For branded goods, use authorized retailers or independently verifiable provenance.

Does a listed product price include shipping and taxes?

Do not assume it does. Check the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, and any applicable taxes or duties separately. Obtain a current quote before committing.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

No downloadable spreadsheet is hosted here. This page provides category links and a practical comparison framework that you can copy into your own spreadsheet.
